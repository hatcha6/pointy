دفتر

Brand Identity

A دفتر is the ledger every Libyan shop already keeps. The product is that ledger, doing the arithmetic — so the promise is not a feeling, it is a claim you can check: the numbers come out right.

Almost all of this identity already ships, in four places that were built separately and have started to drift. This page names one canonical set of values, says which file owns each, and marks what is still open.

pointy is load-bearing: pointy_frontend, the pointy-relay binary, X-Pointy-Relay-Token. Renaming it breaks deploys and protocol headers. The rename was user-facing only, and stays that way.

The tagline
دُوّن في دفتر

A pun: دَوَّن (to enter into a ledger) on دفتر. It is a closing stamp — the foot of a printed slip, the end card of a film, under the mark on a profile. Never a headline, never inside the app. The voweling is fixed at one mark per letter: the PDF shaper drops a kasra tucked under a shadda, so دُوِّنَ mis-renders. Do not "correct" it.

The promise

marketing/BRAND.md § 2

Chosen against the field. The systems competing with us in Libya are fat clients writing straight to a database, and their accounting is wrong in ways their customers can feel but not prove. Everything we say ladders back to a number that reconciles — and to exactly one of these four.

الأرقام تتطابق

The receipt, the Z report, the valuation and the payroll sheet all agree. Show the arithmetic; someone will check it.

يشتغل بدون إنترنت

One backend on the shop's own LAN. Generators and outages are the normal case, not the edge case.

عربي أولاً

Not a translated English layout. The shopkeeper's own Arabic, including the dialect.

يتكامل مع اللي عندك

The fingerprint terminal, the printer, the scanner, the tablet in the drawer. We do not sell hardware.

Never say
  • Cloud, ERP, "digital transformation"
  • Offline sync — we deliberately have none; it is the rivals' bug class
  • A competitor's name
  • "Official vs parallel" of an exchange rate — the axis is how you settle
  • Any AI claim beyond what the model does on a shop's own data
Say instead
  • The figure, with its working shown
  • One backend, on your own network
  • What the product will not do without you
  • Cash or صك — the price you actually pay at
  • Reads the invoice, drafts the order, waits for your approval

Voice

promo/POSTS.md · VOICEOVER.md
  1. Say the number.«24 يوم حضور من 26، يومين غياب −200.00 د.ل» beats "smart attendance tracking". Every figure on a surface must reconcile with every other figure on it.
  2. The shopkeeper's Arabic, not the press release's.«شنو أكثر صنف مبيعاً هذا الأسبوع؟» — not «ما هو المنتج الأكثر مبيعاً خلال الأسبوع الحالي؟»
  3. One idea per sentence, one claim per surface.If a poster needs two headlines, it is two posters.
  4. Name the limit.«ما يُحفظ شيء قبل ما تراجعه وتوافق» is voice, not a disclaimer. Stating what the product will not do on its own is what makes the rest believable.
  5. Never sell against a named competitor.Half our future customers are somebody's customer today. Claims are about our behaviour, never their product.
Arabic typography law — app, print, posters, films, kiosk
No letter-spacing. Ever.Positive tracking breaks connected Arabic. The ramp sets letterSpacing: 0 globally — never undo it locally. Uppercase tracking is for Latin labels only.
Western digitsIn UI, prices and reports.
Money inherits RTLForcing LTR on a field containing د.ل mangles it. Isolate the numeral if you must, never the whole phrase.
Two decimalsOurs. Three (75.000) only when reproducing someone else's Libyan document.
Tabular figuresAnything in a column.

The mark

assets/branding/ · posters/kit.tsx

A tilted page with a second page behind it, and a pencil with a single amber band, on a rounded-square teal ground. Page + pencil = دفتر + دُوّن. That amber band is the only warm note in the mark, and it is why amber is the accent everywhere else.

Lockup geometry — already in code
Gap, mark to wordmark0.34 × mark size
Wordmark size0.72 × mark size
Corner radius0.235 × mark size
Clearspace¼ of the mark's width, all sides
Minimum24 px mark · 40 px lockup
Never
  • Recolour it, or add a gradient or glow
  • Set it on a busy photograph
  • Place it on top of a device or product
  • Stretch it, or rebuild the wordmark in another face
  • Let it become the largest thing in the frame

The logo sits quietly in a corner. The product is the hero.

One sanctioned colour exception. The profile avatar's ground is a flat fill of the artwork's own teal #0C6D69, so the icon's rounded corners dissolve into it and a circular crop has nothing to cut. That value is sampled from the mark, for that purpose only. It is not the brand teal.

Colour

shared/design/pointy_colors.dart

One file is the palette. Everything else mirrors it and does not get its own opinion. Click any swatch to copy its value.

Light
Dark — mirrored token for token
The neutrals are warm

#F8F7F4, #F1EFEA, #E5E0D8, #D5CFC4 are paper, not grey. This is the most identifying thing about the palette and the easiest to lose — a cool #F5F5F5 anywhere reads as a different product.

Teal acts, amber counts

Teal is reserved for what the user can do and what succeeded. Amber is money and attention, never decoration. If a surface needs a third colour to make sense, the layout is wrong.

Grounds — marketing only, two and only two
ink
A lit room for the product to float in. Objects live here: devices, paper, hardware.
paper
The app's own page colour. Ideas live here: lists, claims, arithmetic.

Alternating them down a feed is what gives a grid rhythm. The dark stage palette never ships inside the app.

Open drift

Rules

Headlines are broken by hand with \n — a line left to wrap produces a one-word orphan, and no headline runs longer than two written lines. Emphasis is weight, size and the teal. Never tracking, never italics (Arabic has none), never all-caps.

The one typeface exception

Anything explicitly not ours — a legacy till, a supplier's invoice — is set in 'Geeza Pro', Tahoma, Arial. The moment someone else's document is set in IBM Plex it reads as our artwork instead of their paper.

Pictures and props

marketing/promo/README.md
A real fragment, enlargedA piece of the actual interface at a size no phone ever shows it. That is what separates a poster from a screenshot. Recreated screens are colour-accurate to the shipping app or they are not honest.
Numbers reconcile20.00 − 3.00 = 17.00. 4,285.50 ÷ 187 = 22.92. 2,600.00 − 200.00 + 225.00 = 2,625.00.
Props are drawn and fictionalWe never photograph a real shop's invoice — it would publish a real business's prices, phone number and customer. A prop depicting a capability we have not proven is a promise; don't draw it.
A competitor is an archetypeNo vendor's name, mark or copied layout. The claim is about how much is on screen at once, never about anyone's software being bad.
A third-party mark names a productIt never implies a partnership. ZKTeco's logo appears in one place, on the "works with" chip, on the dark ground its reversed artwork was drawn for. If the integration stops being true, the chip goes.
